Jollibee: Bringing JOY to you

Welcome to Jollibee, the Philippines' favorite fast food chain.

 

Our menu serves up many of your favorite comfort foods, including fried chicken, burgers, spaghetti, french fries, and pies. Try our best selling  and world famous Chickenjoy, our well-loved Jolly Spaghetti, our beefy & juicy Yumburger and our crowd-favorite Peach Mango Pie. Explore our menu for more of Philippines' most-craved fast food meals at budget friendly prices and discover why we are known to be the country's favorite fast food restaurant.

 

So bring your family, friends or even eat solo at a Jollibee near you. You can dine-in with us, drive-thru our restaurants, or have your food for take out or delivery. Wherever you are, we commit our best to serving your delicious meals, bundled with joy every time.
