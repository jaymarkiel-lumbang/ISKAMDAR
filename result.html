How to Avoid Job & Investment Scams

Protect yourself from online fraud by staying alert to common warning signs and adopting simple safety habits:

  • Verify the source: Research the hiring manager, company or broker on official sources, such as the SEC and DOLE/DMW registries, the company's own website and LinkedIn, before taking action.

  • Beware of guaranteed returns: Treat any promise of guaranteed profits or high payouts for minimal work as an immediate red flag.

  • Never pay upfront: Legitimate employers will never ask you to pay for equipment, processing or onboarding, and real investments never require urgent money transfers.

  • Keep communication on-platform: Avoid moving conversations to private messaging apps like Telegram or WhatsApp for initial hiring interviews or financial transactions.

  • Check the links: Inspect website URLs carefully for typos, extra letters or unusual extensions (e.g. .xyz or .top) designed to mimic legitimate sites.
